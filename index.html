How much water to use

Water slowly until it drains out of the bottom of the pot, then empty the saucer. Small sips keep only the top layer wet and leave roots dry.

Signs you are overwatering

Yellow lower leaves, soft stems, and soil that stays wet for days are the common signs. Read our guide to houseplant light too, since low light slows how fast soil dries.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I water houseplants?

Check the soil first. Most houseplants need water when the top inch is dry, which is usually every 7 to 14 days.

What are the signs of overwatering?

Yellow lower leaves, soft stems, soil that stays wet for days, and a sour smell from the pot.
